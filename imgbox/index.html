ImgBox Privacy Policy

App: ImgBox (Android) · Package: com.imgbox.app
Last updated: 16 November 2026

ImgBox is a free app that turns images into shareable links and codes (Direct URL, Markdown, HTML, BBCode) and converts images to PNG, JPG, WEBP, PDF and Base64 files. This policy explains how the app handles your information. It applies only to the ImgBox Android app.

1. Summary

  • No sign-up or account.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob.
  • We do not run our own servers and do not collect, upload or sell your personal information ourselves.
  • Images are uploaded only when you tap Upload, and only to the image host you set up (ImgBB).

2. Images you choose

You pick images from your gallery or files, or take a photo with the camera. The app reads only the images you choose. A copy of each image and a small preview are stored on your device so you can see your history, copy codes and convert files. Converted files are saved to the Downloads/ImgBox folder on your device.

3. Camera

When you tap Take Photo, the app opens your phone's camera app. The photo is saved on your device and used only for the upload you started.

4. Uploading and public links (ImgBB)

If you add your own ImgBB API key in Settings, images you upload are sent to ImgBB (imgbb.com) to create a public link. Anyone who has that link can view the image. ImgBB's handling of uploaded images is covered by the ImgBB privacy policy. Your API key is stored only on your device. If you do not add a key, nothing is uploaded and the app gives you a Base64 code instead of a link.

5. Sharing

When you tap Share or Copy, the link or code goes to the app or clipboard you choose. We do not see what you share.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), app interactions with ads, and diagnostic data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Learn more at How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Data we collect

We, the developer, do not collect any personal data. Data processed by third parties is limited to what is described in sections 4 (ImgBB, only when you upload) and 6 (Google AdMob). All network transfers use encrypted HTTPS connections.

8. Deleting your data

Delete an image from the app (open it and tap the delete button) to remove it from your device, or uninstall the app to remove all its data. Images already uploaded to ImgBB can be deleted from your ImgBB account at imgbb.com.

9. Children

ImgBox is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
