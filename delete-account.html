Delete your NovaPrompt account

App: NovaPrompt (Android) · Package: com.pushugujjar.novapromptapp

Option 1: inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open NovaPrompt and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.
  4. Sign in with Google once more when asked, to confirm it is you.

Option 2: by email

If you can no longer open the app, email pushugujjar4@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "NovaPrompt account deletion". We will delete your data and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your saved favorites
  • Your profile record (name, email, profile photo link, dates)
  • Your sign-in account in NovaPrompt

Deleting your NovaPrompt account does not delete your Google account.
