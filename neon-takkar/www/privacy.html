NEON TAKKAR — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Playing offline / as a guest

Guest and offline play stores your profile (name, level, coins, settings, match history) only on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers.

Photos, characters and custom sounds

Images you upload in the Character Studio and audio you add in the Meme Sound Studio are processed and stored only on your device. They are never uploaded. Please only use images and audio you own or have permission to use.

Google Sign-In and online play

If you sign in with Google we receive your Google account ID, display name, email and profile photo URL from Google. We store a public game profile (player name, player ID, level, avatar emoji, frame, win statistics) so friends and opponents can see it, plus your coins, items and friend list. Your email is not shown to other players.

Online rooms store match state, room chat and reactions temporarily; rooms are deleted automatically after a few hours. Direct messages between friends are stored until you delete your account.

Safety & moderation

Chat messages are automatically filtered for abusive language, links and phone numbers. You can block and report players; reports (reporter, reported player, reason, short context) are reviewed by moderators.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account removes your local data and — if signed in — your online profile, friends, requests and Firebase account. Contact: puspendergmail@gmail.com.
