NovaPrompt Privacy Policy

App: NovaPrompt (Android) · Package: com.pushugujjar.novapromptapp
Last updated: 30 October 2026

NovaPrompt is an app for browsing, saving and copying AI image prompts. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. It applies only to the NovaPrompt Android app.

1. Information we collect

You sign in with your Google account. When you do, the app receives and stores the following:

  • Name shown on your Google account (display name)
  • Email address of your Google account
  • Profile photo link of your Google account, if you have one
  • A unique account ID created by Firebase Authentication
  • Your favorites: the prompts you save inside the app
  • Account creation and update time

We do not ask for your password. Google handles the sign-in. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, or your phone number.

2. How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in
  • To show your name and photo on your profile screen
  • To keep your favorites so they are available when you sign in again, including on a new phone
  • To keep the app secure and prevent abuse (see Google services below)

We do not sell your information. We do not show advertising. We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.

3. Services we use

NovaPrompt uses Google Firebase to run the app. Firebase may process data as described in Google's own policies.

  • Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In: to sign you in
  • Cloud Firestore: to store the prompt library, your profile and your favorites
  • Firebase App Check with Play Integrity: to check that requests come from the genuine app. This can involve device and app integrity signals handled by Google.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Prompt preview pictures are loaded from web links. Your device makes a normal web request to fetch them, and the host of the picture can see standard connection information such as your IP address.

4. Data sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except the service providers listed above who process it on our behalf to run the app, or where the law requires it. Your favorites and profile are private to your account.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase. It is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access rules on the database limit each user to their own profile and favorites. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your profile and favorites until you delete your account.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: open Profile and choose Delete account. This removes your favorites, your profile record and your sign-in account. You will be asked to sign in with Google once more to confirm.

If you cannot open the app, see how to request deletion or email us at the address below.

8. Children

NovaPrompt is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Please check it from time to time.
