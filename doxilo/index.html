Doxilo PDF Privacy Policy

App: Doxilo PDF (Android) · Package: com.doxilo.pdf
Last updated: 13 November 2026

Doxilo PDF is a free app for making PDFs from photos, scanning documents with the camera and merging PDFs. This policy explains how the app handles your information. It applies only to the Doxilo PDF Android app.

1. Summary

  • No sign-up or account.
  • No ads and no analytics.
  • We do not collect, upload or sell your personal information.
  • Your photos and PDFs are processed on your phone and stay on your phone.

2. Photos and files you choose

When you pick photos or PDFs, Android's own picker gives the app access only to the items you select. The app does not browse or read your other files.

The app copies the selected items into its private temporary storage on your phone so it can arrange, rotate and convert the pages. These temporary copies are deleted when you start a new PDF and may also be removed by Android when it clears app cache.

PDFs you create are saved inside the app (shown under Recent Files) and a copy is saved to your phone's Downloads/Doxilo folder. None of this is sent to us or to any server.

3. Camera and document scanner

The Scan feature uses the Google ML Kit Document Scanner, which is provided by Google Play services. It opens Google's scanner screen, processes the images on your device, and returns only the scanned pages to Doxilo PDF. Google Play services may download the scanner module the first time you use it. Google handles that under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

If the scanner is not available on your phone, the app opens your phone's camera app instead, and only the photo you take is returned to Doxilo PDF.

4. Sharing

A PDF is shared only when you tap Share and choose an app (for example WhatsApp or Gmail). What happens after that is controlled by the app you choose.

5. Data we collect

We do not collect any personal or device information. The app has no account system, no advertising, no analytics and no servers of its own.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete any PDF from Recent Files (tap the three dots, then Delete). Uninstalling the app removes all data stored inside the app. Copies in Downloads/Doxilo stay until you delete them with your file manager.

7. Children

Doxilo PDF is a general tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
